## Design Context

### Users
Potters who own a 3D printer, from hobbyists new to printing to makers who know their slicer well.
They come to the tool with a thrown piece (a mug, pot, vase, bowl or plate) and an idea for a
pattern, and they leave with an STL they trust to print first time. They use it at a desk between
studio sessions, often with clay on their hands and a tape measure nearby, so the measurements
and the print check matter more than anything decorative.

### Brand Personality
Warm, calm, tactile. It should feel like a well-kept pottery studio: unhurried, quietly confident,
and on your side. The tool's job is to stop a wasted print, so its voice is plain and specific,
never alarmist. It celebrates a print that's ready, and explains a problem with what to do next.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Studio craft: clay, slip, and glaze tones. Warm, tinted neutrals rather than grey. One glaze-like
  accent (the existing deep teal) used sparingly for actions and "ready" states.
- Light and dark themes, following the system. Dark should feel like a kiln-lit studio at night,
  warm and low, not a neon slicer.
- The designs, the flat stencil and the 3D piece are the stars. Chrome stays quiet around them.
- Anti-references: generic SaaS dashboards, cyan-on-dark "AI" palettes, glassmorphism, card grids
  of identical tiles, gradient text.

### Design Principles
1. **Progressive disclosure.** The simple path (piece → design → check → download) is always
   obvious. Expert controls live one step away, clearly signposted, never hidden.
2. **The work is the hero.** Previews get the space and the contrast. Controls support them.
3. **Speak like a potter's friend who prints.** Plain words, real units (mm, °), and every warning
   says what to do about it.
4. **Trust through honesty.** Show exactly what will print, flag what won't, and never claim
   success the stencil hasn't earned.
5. **Accessible by default.** WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast in both themes, full keyboard use,
   visible focus, labelled controls, and reduced motion respected.
